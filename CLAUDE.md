# Site rules

Work from the latest default branch (`main`). Open a preview pull request for every change. Callum approves merges. Do not merge without that approval.

No em dashes or en dashes in copy, code, or chat replies. Use full stops, commas or colons. Write ranges as "to".

Do not invent reviews, ratings, years in business, accreditations, guarantees, insurance claims, prices or job examples. Use only what is published on the site.

Brand: D & P Roofing and Building. Canvas #0A0F16, surface #141B25, ink #EEF2F6, accent #DE213A, blue #0354B4. Display font Archivo, text font Geist.

The chatbot is edited in `lib/business.ts`.
