Good to know.

Do you charge for a quote?+

No. Quotes are free and there's no obligation, whether it's a few slipped tiles or a full re-roof.

Will the price change once you start?+

We give you a clear, detailed quote up front and stick to it. In one customer's words: "completed to a very high standard and exactly at the price we were quoted." (Nick Wilder)

Do you use subcontractors?+

No. We don't use outside contractors. Our own roofing, building and groundworks teams do the work.

Do you organise scaffolding and skips?+

Yes. We organise the scaffold and skips and deal with building control where it's needed.

Is the work guaranteed?+

All work is fully guaranteed. Re-roofs come with a 10 year guarantee.

Do you do emergency work?+

Yes. For leaks and storm damage, ring 07587 415299. Out of hours, send us a WhatsApp with a photo and your postcode and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

When can I reach you?+

Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm, Saturday and Sunday 10am to 4pm. Send a WhatsApp any time and we'll get back to you.
